What are Terms & Conditions?
A legal agreement between you and your users. Sets the rules — what they can do, what they can't, and what happens if things go wrong.
Why do I need them?
Without T&C you have zero legal protection. If someone abuses your platform or steals your content, you have nothing to stand on.
What is a Liability Clause?
Limits how much you can be held responsible if something goes wrong. Protects you from unlimited legal exposure.
What is Arbitration?
Means disputes are resolved by a neutral third party instead of going to court. Faster and cheaper than a lawsuit.
What is Intellectual Property?
Your content — logo, text, code, design. T&C makes clear it all belongs to you and cannot be copied without permission.
