What are Cookies?
Tiny files websites save on your browser. They remember if you are logged in, your preferences, and your location. Not dangerous — but they collect data.
What is a Cookie Policy?
A page telling users which cookies you use and why. If your site has Google Analytics or any ads, you need this.
What is GDPR?
A European law that requires you to get user consent before placing non-essential cookies on their browser.
What is the ePrivacy Directive?
Also called the Cookie Law. Requires websites to get clear consent from users before using tracking cookies.
Strictly Necessary Cookies?
Cookies your site cannot work without — like keeping users logged in. These don't need consent. All others do.

Why a Cookie Policy?
GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive, and laws like CCPA and LGPD require websites that use cookies to clearly disclose what cookies they use and why. A Cookie Policy helps you stay compliant and builds trust with visitors.
