What is a EULA?
A legal contract between you and the end user defining exactly how your software or app may be used — and what users cannot do with it.
Do I need one?
Yes — if you distribute an app or software. Apple App Store and Google Play both require a EULA before listing your app.
EULA vs Terms & Conditions
T&Cs govern use of a service. A EULA specifically governs the software license — what users can and cannot do with your code.
What does it protect?
Your IP, limits liability, prevents reverse engineering, and sets the rules for termination if a user breaches the agreement.
