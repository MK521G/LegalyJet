The real problem with most free generators

Most free Privacy Policy generators give you a generic document full of placeholder text like [YOUR COMPANY NAME], [INSERT DATE], and [DESCRIBE YOUR DATA PRACTICES HERE]. You then have to manually find and replace every placeholder, which takes time and is easy to get wrong.

The result is often a document that looks like a template — because it is — and does not accurately reflect your actual data practices. That can be worse than no policy at all, because it creates a false impression.

What paid options offer

Paid services like Termly, iubenda, and GetTerms typically charge $10–$30 per month. What you get for that price varies, but often includes:

  • A more detailed questionnaire to capture your specific data practices
  • Automatic updates when privacy laws change
  • Cookie consent banner integration
  • Multi-language support
  • A hosted URL for your policy

For a large business with complex data practices operating across multiple jurisdictions, a paid service can be worth it for the automatic update feature alone.

What actually matters for most websites

For most small websites, indie apps, and side projects, what matters is:

  • The policy accurately describes your actual data practices
  • It covers the key legal requirements — GDPR, CCPA, CalOPPA
  • It is accessible from every page of your site
  • It includes your real contact information

You do not need to pay $30 a month for that. You need a generator that asks you the right questions and uses your answers to create a document that reflects what your site actually does.
