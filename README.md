## What is LegalyJet?

I was building a side project and needed a Privacy Policy before I could submit it to the app store. Every free generator I tried either gave me a wall of placeholder text that I had to manually fill in, or charged $50 for something basic. I just wanted something that asked me a few questions about my app and wrote the whole thing for me — personalized, professional, ready to publish. That didn't exist for free, so I built it.

LegalyJet takes under 4 minutes, uses your actual business details throughout the document, covers GDPR and CCPA, and costs nothing. No account, no watermarks, no lawyer fees.
